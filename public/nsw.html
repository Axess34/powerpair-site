Questions business owners ask

Do I need solar for the NSW business discount?

No. The NSW discount applies to battery-only installs too, but it is larger if you add new solar (at least a quarter of the battery size, installed within 90 days). The federal battery rebate does need solar on site.

Is the Bill Audit really free?

Yes. You get your numbers whether or not you go ahead. If you ask for a quote, the installer we pair you with pays us a referral fee; it does not change your price.

Are these government grants?

No. They are government-backed rebates and discounts. Your accredited installer takes them off the quote. Nobody applies for a cash grant, and anyone who says otherwise is worth avoiding.

Why does install date matter?

The federal battery rebate is set by the install date, not the order date. It steps down on 1 January 2027 and again on 1 July 2027. Small solar systems also drop from 5 to 4 years of certificates on 1 January 2027.

How accurate is the calculator?

It is a first estimate from one number. The Bill Audit uses your actual usage, tariff and demand charges, and an installer quote is the final word.

Do you arrange the finance?

No. We show what a loan could look like so you can compare it with your bill. Your bank, an equipment financier or the installer's finance partner sets the actual rate, fees and approval. Ask about green or CEFC-backed business loans, which can carry a discount. "Keep the asset" applies to a loan or chattel mortgage, where your business owns the system. Under a power purchase agreement or rental, the provider owns it.

Who will contact me?

PowerPair first, to check the details. Then one accredited installer, only if you ask for a quote.
